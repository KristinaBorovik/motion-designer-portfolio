About

Motion designer with 2+ years of client work, looking for a team to grow with.

I work mainly in 2D — logo animation, motion identity and UI animation — and take projects into 3D when the idea needs volume, light or texture. Most of my work so far has been for branding, tech, iGaming and creative clients, delivered fully remotely.

I'm now looking for a full-time role or an ongoing partnership rather than one-off projects. Repeat work is where motion actually gets good: you learn the product, build a system, and every new asset takes less time and looks more like the brand.

I'm based in Montenegro on Central European Time (UTC+1/+2) — the same working hours as most of Europe, one hour ahead of the UK, and a solid overlap with US East Coast mornings.
